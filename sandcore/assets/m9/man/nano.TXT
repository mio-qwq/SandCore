SandNano - serial and graphical-terminal source editor

  nano [-v] [file]

Ctrl-O saves through a SandFS transaction; a failed save keeps the buffer.
Ctrl-X exits and asks about unsaved changes. Ctrl-W searches literal text.
Ctrl-G shows keys. Ctrl-K cuts a line; Ctrl-U pastes. Alt-U/Alt-E undo/redo.
-v opens read-only. UTF-8 and the file's CRLF/LF bytes are preserved.
Use the host serial tool's :raw mode for individual keys.

Limits: 4 MiB document, 131072 indexed lines, 32 undo entries / 8 MiB.
Source implemented; M9 behavior and performance are not yet verified.
